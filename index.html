System · Alaya Lab 2026

MIO

A digital human for live streaming and AI companionship

Full demo video

A conversational digital human served through an OpenAI-compatible API, so it plugs into custom front ends, companion devices, and live-stream interaction. Its memory keeps growing with every conversation.

  • Qwen3-8B SFT controller
  • Persona · experience · knowledge stores
  • EverMemOS long-term memory
  • Expression-to-motion avatar
System · Alaya Lab 2026

Synthetic World

A self-evolving AI community

Live demo

Users define a world and its characters; AI residents post, reply, and react as events unfold. Visitors can watch, or steer the story with "Next Act". Past events and character states shape what happens next.

  • Structured LLM generation
  • Persistent event state
  • Scheduled community updates
  • iOS · Android · Web

HFP-SAM: Hierarchical Frequency Prompted SAM for Efficient Marine Animal Segmentation

Pingping Zhang, Tianyu Yan, Yuhao Wang, Yang Liu, et al.

A wavelet frequency prior steers a frozen SAM: FGA injects frequency masks into lightweight adapters, FPS turns high-frequency windows into point prompts, and Full-View Mamba refines the mask at linear cost.

PaperCodeResults
CVPR 2024 · HighlightCCF-AStudent-first

Fantastic Animals and Where to Find Them: Segment Any Marine Animal with Dual SAM

Pingping Zhang, Tianyu Yan, Yang Liu, Huchuan Lu

Two SAM branches see the original and a gamma-corrected image, linked by multi-level coupled prompts. DFAM fuses their features, the two decoders supervise each other with pseudo-labels, and C3P predicts each pixel's criss-cross connectivity instead of a plain mask.
